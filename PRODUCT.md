# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Muslims looking for an elegant, distraction-free companion to organize and complete their daily spiritual practices (Quran reading, prayers tracking, Athkar supplication, and Islamic Q&A). The app is used in varying lighting conditions (especially low-light during Dawn/Fajr and Night/Isha prayers), requiring high-contrast legibility, ambient-lit aesthetics, and quick offline access.

## Product Purpose
ImanHub serves as an offline-first, feature-rich Islamic companion application. Success looks like providing Muslims with elegant, fluid utility (prayer times calculation, Quran reading logs, tasbeeh counts, and Qibla tracking) in a premium, ad-free container that promotes spiritual focus and mindfulness.

## Brand Personality
Spiritual, Elegant, Glassmorphic

## Anti-references
- Cluttered layouts with intrusive banner ads, donation popups, or irrelevant news widgets.
- Generic, flat bright white themes and basic primary colors that feel corporate rather than serene/spiritual.
- Overstimulating animations or aggressive popup notification behaviors that disrupt quiet reflection.

## Design Principles
- **Spiritual Calm:** Layouts must feel open, quiet, and deliberate. Spacing and typography should offer breathing room, echoing the peace of a prayer space.
- **Ambient Context:** Visual elements (background flows, active rings, glows) must adapt dynamically to match the current prayer stage or category.
- **Glassmorphic Precision:** Translucent surfaces, frosted glass containers, and neon/pastel glows are used with purpose to create a high-end, premium digital sanctuary.
- **Zero Distraction:** Clean hierarchy, ad-free screens, and fast offline interactions. Utility serves user focus.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard WCAG 2.1 AA compliance. Provide high contrast text options for low-light legibility, support system font scaling for calligraphic texts, and ensure haptic interactions offer clear non-visual feedback.
