## 🚀 **HOW TO USE THE APP**

### **Prayer Times**
1. Grant location permission
2. View today's prayer times
3. See which prayer is next (highlighted in green)

### **Quran**
1. Browse Surahs
2. Tap to read (swiping pages left/right)
3. Use the **Bookmark** icon in the header to save your place

### **Athkar**
1. Choose a category
2. Tap the card to count
3. Swipe to next Athkar
